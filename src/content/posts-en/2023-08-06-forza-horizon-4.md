*Connecting to Horizon Life.*

The taxi missions are genuinely fun, though the story mode repeats itself a lot, and the combo skill chains are absurd — pure player torture.

Forza Horizon 4 made a dream real for me. I will never get to drive a supercar in real life, and I will never get to pull off those stunts for real. In this game I can stand in the snow mountains where the aurora comes down to meet me, and watch Edinburgh turn through spring, summer, autumn and winter.

The soundtrack is the best game BGM I have ever heard. That opening — *A Moment Apart* — is pure fantasy, and I never get tired of it. When I am just cruising around, I switch to the Timeless station. The blue Senna starts to spin, and the world goes from the bright green of spring to the saturated colour of summer, the golden leaves of autumn, and finally the ice and snow of winter. That is the charm this game brings.

If you like speed and the heart-pounding rush of the limit, pick a hypercar, load a top-speed tune, and floor it on the motorway. If you like drifting — an RX-7, a 458 — and the feeling of a wild horse running free, head to Fortune Island and take on the mountain slopes. And even if you do nothing at all, you can cruise slowly around Great Britain: find the scenic spots, the pink flower fields, the cherry-blossom avenue, open photo mode and pick an angle. Driving slowly is how you actually see the scenery along the way. Every road here is worth driving.

Honestly, single-player is great too — just run one more "never-ending festival".
